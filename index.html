About me

I'm a Cebu-based student currently studying Information Technology at Cebu Eastern College, Incorporated. I became interested in this course because I like seeing how code and devices can be used to solve everyday problems for real people.

I enjoy building small projects, learning how sensors and cameras work with software, and designing simple, easy-to-use websites. Outside class, I like watching tech videos and playing games.
